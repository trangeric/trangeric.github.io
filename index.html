Classic Car Parking Light

This original 1970 Firebird parking lamp used a reflector-style halogen bulb. The goal was to modernize the assembly while maintaining OEM fitment.

The housing was redesigned around the OEM connector and upgraded with an LED bulb and halo ring.

The final product is CNC-machined from 6061 aluminum with chrome or gloss black finishes.

Drop-in Replacement Boards

The original housing used halogen bulbs and a diffraction lens. This redesign modernizes the tail light with innovative LED patterns and improved brightness.

The housing was 3D scanned and modeled to develop PCB boards and internal mounting solutions.

Final assemblies are fully drop-in replacements requiring no modification to OEM parts.

The animation showcases a cycling preview of all 13 turn signal patterns.

3D Printed Headlight Bucket

Many classic vehicles used shallow buckets that cannot fit modern LED headlights without modification.

This 3D-printed bucket replaces the OEM design and incorporates threaded inserts for easy installation.

The redesigned bucket offers a low-cost, high-margin solution to support modern lighting upgrades.

Volvo P1800 Tail Lights

A custom lighting assembly designed for a fully restored Volvo P1800.

Inspired by modern Ferrari circular tail lights, the design incorporates segmented turn signal patterns and a centered reverse light.

To stay within budget, 3D-printed supports were used to mount the PCBs and create the signature lighting pattern.

Acrylic lenses were CNC machined and painted, with PCBs developed collaboratively with our electrical engineer.

Final installation showcased at a public automotive event.
